Understanding Your Subtle Biofield: What Is Aura Cleansing?

By Deepika•14 November 2026•4 min read

Have you ever walked into a crowded commuter train or a stressful office meeting and felt completely drained within minutes, even though you hadn't exerted physical effort?

In holistic and spiritual traditions, this phenomenon is understood through the subtle biofield or **aura**—the electromagnetic energy envelope that surrounds every living being.

How Our Energy Field Accumulates Stagnancy Just as our physical skin collects dust and environmental elements throughout the day, our subtle energy field constantly interacts with the emotional and mental vibrations of the spaces we inhabit.

When you spend long periods around conflict, unresolved stress, or emotionally demanding situations, your energetic boundary can feel weighed down, sluggish, or cluttered.

What Happens During an Aura Cleansing Session? At Inner Mirror Bliss, an Aura Cleansing session (₹300 / 30 mins) is a peaceful, restorative experience designed to help you hit the energetic reset button:

  • **Non-Invasive Sweeping:** Using traditional consecrated tools and subtle intention to dissolve stagnant vibrations around your head, shoulders, and chest.
  • **Detaching Stress Cords:** Gently releasing the feeling of being pulled in ten directions at once.
  • **Luminous Boundary Sealing:** Concluding with high-frequency positive intentions so you feel contained, grounded, and refreshed.

Simple Daily Practices to Keep Your Energy Light Between formal sessions, here are three simple practices you can do at home: - **Salt Baths or Foot Soaks:** Warm water with rock salt or Epsom salt naturally grounds electrical and mental fatigue. - **Mindful Shower Visualizations:** As water rinses over you, visualize any stress from your day washing down the drain. - **Conscious Breathing:** Taking five slow, deep diaphragmatic breaths whenever you step between work and home.
