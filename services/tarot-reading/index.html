1. INTRODUCTION & OVERVIEW

About Tarot Card Reading

Deepika conducts an empathetic, deeply channeled 60-minute Tarot consultation. Whether you seek resolution in love and relationships, career direction, financial decisions, or personal growth, this session serves as a luminous mirror to guide your decisions with serenity and confidence.

What to Expect During Your Session

  • ✓A quiet, respectful, and private space dedicated entirely to your personal questions.
  • ✓A grounded explanation of the cards drawn, their symbolism, and their practical resonance.
  • ✓An open dialogue where you can ask clarifying questions throughout the full hour.
  • ✓Actionable reflections and empowering perspectives to take with you into your daily life.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About Tarot Card Reading

Is Tarot about predicting the future with absolute certainty?↓

No. At Inner Mirror Bliss, tarot is approached as a tool for personal clarity, self-reflection, and perspective. The cards reflect current energies, emotional patterns, and potential trajectories, while honoring that your free will and daily actions always determine your future.

How long does a session last and what should I prepare?↓

The session lasts a full 60 minutes (₹599). You don't need any prior experience. Simply bring a curious mind and, if you like, a notebook and 2–3 questions or life areas you wish to explore.

Can I attend online via video call or is it only in-person?↓

Both options are available! You can visit our peaceful studio in Ghansoli, Navi Mumbai, or book a private online video consultation from anywhere in India or abroad.

Is what we discuss kept confidential?↓

Absolutely. Everything shared during your tarot reading is strictly confidential and held in complete privacy and respect.
